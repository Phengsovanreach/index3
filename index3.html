Our Story

From ancient Ethiopian highlands to your cup, coffee has been bringing people together for centuries.

The Journey Begins

Legend tells of Kaldi, an Ethiopian goat herder who discovered coffee when he noticed his goats becoming energetic after eating certain berries. This magical discovery would eventually travel across continents, creating cultures and communities around the simple act of sharing a cup.

Today, we continue this tradition by sourcing the finest beans from sustainable farms worldwide, roasting them to perfection, and serving them with the passion that coffee deserves.
